Privacy

Privacy information

This site is a simple marketing website for Whites Exterior Cleaning. It does not use analytics, ad tracking or embedded marketing cookies at the time of writing.

What information may be collected

If you contact Whites Exterior Cleaning by phone, email or WhatsApp, the information you choose to send may include your name, address, contact details and details about the work you want quoted.

How the site handles enquiries

The contact form on the main page opens your email app with a prepared draft. The website itself does not store the message contents on the page.

Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. If this changes later and analytics or other services are added, this page should be updated to reflect that.
